# Driver-Drowsiness-Detection-System
It is a system that calculate the Eyes Aspect Ratio and Mouth Aspect Ratio, to tell if the driver is feeling sleepy or not. 

Driver Drowsiness Detection System is an important technology designed to improve road
safety and reduce the number of accidents caused by drowsy driving. Drowsy driving is a serious
issue that affects millions of people worldwide, and it can have deadly consequences. Fatigue
can impair a driver's judgment, reaction time, and decision-making abilities, leading to serious
accidents and even fatalities. To address this issue, the Driver Drowsiness Detection System has
been developed.
This system works by continuously monitoring the driver's behavior and physiological markers,
such as eye movement and head position, to detect any signs of drowsiness. The system uses
cameras, sensors, or other devices to collect data, which is then analyzed in real-time to
determine if the driver is becoming drowsy. If the system determines that the driver is indeed
tired, it sounds an alarm or vibrates the seat to alert them and prevent them from falling asleep
at the wheel. The aim of this technology is to prevent accidents and save lives by keeping
drivers alert and focused on the road.
The Driver Drowsiness Detection System is easy to use and can be integrated into most modern
vehicles. It operates automatically, without the need for any manual intervention, so drivers
can simply concentrate on driving. The system is also highly accurate, with a low false-positive
rate, meaning that it will only alert drivers if they are actually drowsy and in need of a break.
In conclusion, the Driver Drowsiness Detection System is a critical technology that can save
lives and improve road safety. With the number of accidents caused by drowsy driving on the
rise, it is more important than ever to adopt this technology and keep our roads safe for
everyone. Whether you're a professional truck driver or a weekend road tripper, the Driver
Drowsiness Detection System is an essential tool for ensuring that you stay awake, alert, and
focused on the road at all times.
